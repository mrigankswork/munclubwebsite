About The Club

"

Service Before Self guides our delegation as we compete — and win — at the most prestigious conferences across the nation.

Club Motto

Welcome to the official Model United Nations Club of Delhi Public School, Noida. Since its establishment in 2004, the club has produced some of the most outspoken, eloquent, and articulate MUNers and has successfully garnered over 580 Individual Awards and over 100 School Delegation Awards.

We hope to strive for the best in every domain of this activity. Our delegates represent a tradition of excellence, critical thinking, and diplomatic finesse that has been carefully cultivated over two decades.
